One line, in the voice of the Moon replying at last to the wishes, howls
and late-night promises of everyone staring up at it.

> "I've heard four billion years of wishes, howls, and 2 a.m. promises — and not one of you ever asked how I was. I kept the light on anyway."

## Entry

`main.py` (Python 3, standard library only) writes that single line to
`$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt` (default `/output/entry.txt`). The line is
138 characters — inside the 1–240 character gate, no wasted characters.
